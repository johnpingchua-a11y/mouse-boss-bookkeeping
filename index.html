Staff & access / 员工与权限

Owner: full access. Staff: record sales, expenses and stock. This preview cannot enforce secure roles.

For the live website, staff accounts must be created through Supabase Authentication and assigned Owner or Staff roles. Never share one password among staff.

Recommended roles

  • Owner / 老板：all records, costs, profits, reports, staff and settings.
  • Staff / 员工：sales, customer orders and stock operations; no owner-only profit reports.
Preview mode: data is stored only in this browser on this device. It is not yet shared across phones or staff. Do not enter real customer personal data or rely on this preview as your business ledger.
